MONEY MANAGEMENT APP - COMPLETE FEATURE GUIDE
=============================================

1. USER ROLES & AUTHENTICATION
-------------------------------
- Three user roles: Executive, Accountant, Admin
- Mobile-based login with PIN/password authentication
- Secure JWT token-based session management
- Role-based access control for all features
- Active/inactive user status management

2. EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD & FEATURES
----------------------------------

- Today's collection target display
- Current day collection vs. target
- Assigned companies count
- Top performing companies by payment status

Company Management:
- View all assigned companies with filtering
- Search companies by code, name, or area
- Company details: bills, balances, credit info
- Outstanding amount tracking per company

Bill Management:
- View all pending bills by company
- Multiple bills selection for bulk payment
- Overdue bills tracking with days calculation
- Bill history and payment allocations
- Promise date setting for future payments

Payment Collection:
- Single payment submission with proof
- Multi-payment (bulk) submission across bills
- Payment methods: Cash, Check, UPI, Bank Transfer, NEFT
- Photo proof capture and location tracking
- Real-time amount validation (₹1.00 minimum)
- Auto-location capture or manual entry
- Comment/notes field for payment context

Payment History:
- View all submitted payments (unified history)
- Filter by status: All, Pending, Approved, Declined
- Search payments by company, area, or reference
- Payment status tracking: Submitted → Accountant → Admin
- Payment detail view with full audit trail

5. PAYMENT LIFECYCLE
--------------------
Stage 1 - Executive Submission:
- Executive collects payment and submits with proof
- Status: "Submitted"
- Includes: amount, method, photo, location, comment

Stage 2 - Accountant Review:
- Accountant reviews and approves/declines
- Status: "Accountant Approved" or "Accountant Declined"
- Accountant comment added

Stage 3 - Admin Final Approval:
- Admin reviews accountant-approved payments
- Status: "Admin Approved" or "Admin Declined"
- Admin comment added
- Final disposition

Payment Status Flow:
Submitted → Accountant Approved → Admin Approved (Success)
         → Accountant Declined (Rejected at Stage 2)
                              → Admin Declined (Rejected at Stage 3)
